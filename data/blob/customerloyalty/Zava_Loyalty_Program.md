# Zava Rewards Loyalty Program

Zava Rewards is the customer loyalty program for Zava Retail stores and the Zava online store. Members earn points on every purchase and unlock tier-based discounts, early access to campaigns, and exclusive offers.

## Membership Tiers

| Tier | Annual Spend | Points per $1 | Base Discount Range | Key Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | $0 to $499 | 1 point | 5% to 9.99% | Birthday coupon, member-only emails |
| Silver | $500 to $1,499 | 1.5 points | 10% to 17.99% | Free standard shipping, early sale notifications |
| Gold | $1,500 to $3,999 | 2 points | 18% to 24.99% | Free express shipping, extended 60-day returns |
| Platinum | $4,000 and above | 3 points | 25% to 35% | Personal design consultation, 90-day returns, priority support |

## How the Personalized Discount Is Calculated

Each member receives a personalized discount within the range of their tier. The discount is based on:

- Annual spend within the tier
- Membership tenure (years as a member)
- Purchase frequency over the last 12 months
- Participation in seasonal campaigns

The personalized discount is recalculated on the first day of every quarter.

## Seasonal Campaigns

### Holiday and Black Friday Campaign
- Runs from the Friday after Thanksgiving through the following Monday (Cyber Monday)
- Gold and Platinum members get 24-hour early access
- Tier discount can be combined with Black Friday promotions up to a maximum combined discount of 50%
- Double points on all purchases during the campaign

### Spring Home Refresh Campaign
- Runs for the first two weeks of April
- Extra 5% off paint, flooring, and outdoor living categories for Silver tier and above

## Points Redemption

- 100 points = $1 in Zava Rewards credit
- Minimum redemption: 500 points
- Points expire 24 months after they are earned

## Tier Review

Tiers are reviewed annually on January 1. Members who do not meet the spend threshold move down one tier. Members who exceed the threshold of the next tier are upgraded immediately.
